### 💡 Strategic Data-Backed Recommendations
Based on the exploratory findings, we propose three tactical optimizations:

1.  **Scale Social Acquisition Budgets:** Reallocate marketing spends to prioritize **Instagram** based on its unmatched revenue acquisition metrics[cite: 6].
2.  **Premium Product Bundling:** Maximize cart values by creating high-margin bundles. Group top-performing **Chairs** or **Printers** with lower-performing **Phone** units to drive up the Average Order Value (AOV)[cite: 6].
3.  **Optimize Email Retention Flows:** Leverage the low operational cost of **Email Marketing** to build targeted automation sequences aimed at customers within the core transaction distribution range of **$410 to $1,579**[cite: 6].
